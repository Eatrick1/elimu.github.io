Our Story

From Komamboga
to East Africa

Elimu Foundation Africa Limited was founded in Kawempe Municipality, Kampala, with a simple but powerful vision: to initiate and sustain programs that make a real difference in the lives of the most vulnerable.

Starting in the streets of Komamboga Parish, we began by identifying children at risk of dropping out of school and connecting them with resources, mentors, and community support. Over the years the work expanded to women's empowerment, church partnerships, and youth leadership.

Today, Elimu Foundation Africa operates across multiple communities in Uganda with a growing footprint in East Africa, always returning to its roots of genuine, grassroots, community-led change.

Our Purpose

Elimu Foundation and the SDGs

Our work is deeply rooted in the principles of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. By focusing on poverty alleviation, gender equality, access to education and healthcare, environmental conservation, and economic empowerment, we make a significant impact on the lives of individuals and the broader community in need.

Through these efforts, we strive to create a more equitable and sustainable future for all, while protecting the environment and creating shared value for our planet, our communities, and our partners.

No Poverty Zero Hunger Good Health & Well-Being Quality Education Gender Equality Clean Water & Sanitation Decent Work & Economic Growth Reduced Inequalities Partnerships for the Goals

Looking Ahead: our investment strategy is focused on initiatives that promote environmental conservation, economic development, and social equity for lasting impact. Through education, community engagement, and strategic partnerships, Elimu is building sustainable communities.

Where We're Rooted

Uganda, the Pearl of Africa

Our work is grounded in Uganda, a nation of extraordinary generosity, diversity, and natural richness. Uganda is home to the source of the world's longest river, the Nile, one of the world's most ethnically diverse populations, and the highest density of primates found anywhere on Earth, including half of the world's population of endangered mountain gorillas. It is also one of the world's most affordable safari destinations, with massive wildlife reserves and over 1,066 recorded bird species. In Kampala's own Katwe suburb, the Hollywood film Queen of Katwe told the true story of Ugandan chess prodigy Phiona Mutesi, a reminder of the potential waiting to be unlocked in communities just like the ones we serve.
